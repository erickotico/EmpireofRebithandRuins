      Delete account

      Deleting your account is permanent. Before you continue, see what is deleted and what is kept.

      Deleted right away:

      • Your profile, nickname, bio, crest, badges and date of birth.
      • Your posts, images, comments, likes and answers.
      • Your friends, blocks, notifications and online status.
      • Your private messages, including in the other person's conversation.

      Kept, no longer linked to an account:

      • Copies of content reported against you: up to 1 year after the report is resolved.
      • The moderation record (nickname and action taken): up to 2 years.
      • Technical records of our providers (sign-in, e-mails sent): up to the periods in the Privacy policy.

      For your safety, sign in again before deleting (your last sign-in was more than 15 minutes ago).

      Admin accounts cannot be deleted from the site.

      While your account is suspended or banned, it cannot be deleted. Once the suspension ends you can delete it here; for a ban, write to empireofrebirth@gmail.com.
